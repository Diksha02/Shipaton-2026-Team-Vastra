## The short version

- Your wardrobe lives **on your device** by default. We do not need an account
  for you to use it.
- We do **not sell your personal data**, and we do not share it with advertisers.
- We do **not** use your photographs to train AI models.
- You can **delete anything you have saved, at any time, for free**. Deletion is
  never behind a payment.

## What we collect

### If you use Vastra without an account
- **Wardrobe content** you create: garment photographs, outfit combinations,
  names you give them. Stored **on your device**.
- **Basic diagnostics**: app version, device model, operating system, crash
  reports. Used to fix faults.

### If you sign in
- **Account identifiers** from Google Sign-In: a Firebase user ID, your email
  address, and your display name and profile picture if your Google account
  provides them. **We never receive your Google password.**
- **A referral code** we generate for you, and the code of whoever referred you.

### If you buy something
Purchases are processed by **Apple** or **Google**, and managed through
**RevenueCat**. We receive a purchase receipt, subscription status and an
anonymous or account-linked identifier. **We never receive your card details.**
We do not take card payments directly (no Stripe or Razorpay checkout in Vastra).

### If you post to the Looks feed
- The image you post and any caption.
- Moderation results for that image.

### If you use try-on
- **A photograph of you that you choose to provide.**

> ⚠️ **Read this part carefully.** A photograph you supply for try-on is
> processed to generate an image of you wearing a garment. Depending on where you
> live this may be treated as **biometric information**. We ask for your separate,
> explicit consent before any such processing, and try-on stays switched off until
> you give it. See "Biometric information" below.

## Biometric information

This section applies in addition to everything else, and is written to satisfy
the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14) as well as the
Texas and Washington biometric statutes.

**What we collect.** If — and only if — you enable try-on and give explicit
consent, we process a photograph of you that you supply.

**Why.** Solely to generate an image showing how a garment would look on you, at
your request. For no other purpose.

**Who we share it with.** Our AI processing provider, **Google Cloud Vertex AI**,
acting as our processor under contract. They are **not** permitted to use your
image for their own purposes or to train their models.

**We never sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from biometric information.**

**How long we keep it — our retention and destruction schedule.** We permanently
destroy your try-on photograph and any biometric identifiers derived from it at
the **earliest** of:

1. when you withdraw consent or delete the photograph;
2. when you delete your account;
3. when the purpose for collection has been satisfied; or
4. **three (3) years** after your last interaction with us.

**How to withdraw.** Email vastra.sup@gmail.com, or delete your try-on photo in
the app. This destroys the image and disables try-on. There is no charge, and no
delay.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: **access** your data; **correct** it;
**erase** it; **restrict** or **object to** processing; **portability**; and to
**withdraw consent**. You also have the right not to be discriminated against for
exercising these rights.

If you are a California resident you additionally have the rights to **know**,
**delete**, **correct**, and to **opt out of sale or sharing** — though we do
neither.

**How to exercise them.** Email vastra.sup@gmail.com. We respond within
**one month** (UK) or **45 days** (California). Exercising your rights is free.

## Children

Vastra is not for children under **13**, and if you are in the UK or EU you must
be at least **16** to consent to the optional processing described above. We do
not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us
data, email us and we will delete it.

need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the ICO of a
personal data breach where the law requires it.
