4. Biometric / try-on photographs

If — and only if — you enable try-on and give explicit consent, we process a photograph you supply solely to generate a try-on image at your request.

Our AI processor is Google Cloud Vertex AI, acting under contract. They are not permitted to use your image to train their models for their own purposes. We never sell, lease, trade, or profit from biometric information.

We destroy try-on photographs and derived biometric identifiers at the earliest of:

  1. when you withdraw consent or delete the photograph;
  2. when you delete your account;
  3. when the purpose has been satisfied; or
  4. three (3) years after your last interaction with us.

How to withdraw. Email vastra.sup@gmail.com, or delete your try-on photo in the app. This destroys the image and disables try-on. There is no charge, and no delay.
